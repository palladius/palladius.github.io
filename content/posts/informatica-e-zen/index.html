In Japan, they have replaced the impersonal Microsoft error messages with
Haiku poetry messages. Haiku poetry has strict construction rules. Each
poem has only three lines, 17 syllables: five syllables in the first line,
seven in the second, five in the third. Haiku is used to communicate a
timeless message often achieving a wistful, yearning and
powerful insight through extreme brevity - the essence of Zen:

Your file was so big.
It might be very useful.
But now it is gone.
